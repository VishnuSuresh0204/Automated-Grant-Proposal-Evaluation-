INTELLIGENT ARCHITECTURE

Transforming Grant Evaluation With AI

Traditional grant peer review is slow, prone to cognitive bias, and bogged down by administrative friction. AGPES combines cutting-edge Natural Language Processing with standardized rubrics to make evaluation faster, fairer, and auditable.

Automated NLP Pre-Screening

Analyzes proposal syntax, eligibility compliance, completeness of documentation, and budget calculations within minutes of submission.

  • Plagiarism & originality audit
  • Budget vs work-package coherence
  • Guideline format verification
Smart Reviewer Matching

Semantic topic modeling compares proposal abstracts and keywords against reviewer publication records and expertise domains without conflict of interest.

  • Automated conflict-of-interest detection
  • Workload & reviewer availability balancing
  • Domain relevance score generation
Multi-Criteria Rubric Scoring

Calibrated scoring across Technical Merit, Methodological Rigor, Societal & Economic Impact, and Resource Feasibility.

  • Standardized quantitative rubrics
  • Weighted AI recommendation score
  • Comprehensive qualitative feedback
Double-Blind & Bias Mitigation

Protects researchers from gender, institutional, and geographic evaluation biases through automated anonymization during peer review.

  • Automated entity de-identification
  • Blinded peer review workspace
  • Transparent decision audit log
Real-Time Evaluation Dashboards

Dedicated portals for Applicants, Reviewers, and Funding Administrators with real-time analytics, notifications, and review queues.

  • Applicant lifecycle tracking
  • Reviewer evaluation worksheets
  • Admin cohort ranking & awards
Actionable Feedback Generation

Unsuccessful and funded applicants receive structured, constructive critiques and AI-synthesized improvement suggestions for future cycles.

  • Detailed rubric score breakdown
  • Reviewer consensus commentary
  • Resubmission enhancement hints
END-TO-END WORKFLOW

The AI Evaluation Lifecycle

From initial document upload to final award disbursement, every stage is structured and auditable.

01
Proposal Submission

Applicant fills required metadata, uploads research proposal PDF, budget ledger, and timeline milestones through the Applicant Portal.

02
AI Pre-Evaluation

System runs semantic parsing, compliance check, novelty evaluation, and generates an initial composite AI score (0-100).

03
Expert Peer Review

Matched domain reviewers access anonymized proposals, examine AI suggestions, and grade against calibrated rubrics with detailed notes.

04
Decision & Award

Admin console aggregates AI confidence + reviewer consensus to rank submissions, disburse grants, and issue transparent feedback reports.

SCORING RUBRIC

Standardized, Transparent Rubrics

Our dual-layer scoring model harmonizes AI algorithmic evaluation with human peer reviewer domain judgment across 4 key dimensions:

Technical Innovation & Novelty 30% Weight
Assesses scientific originality, state-of-the-art advancement, and prior-art comparison.
Methodological Rigor & Feasibility 25% Weight
Evaluates experimental plan, team capabilities, timeline, and contingency risk buffers.
Budget Justification & Realism 25% Weight
Cross-checks equipment expenses, personnel costs, and benchmarked market rates.
Societal & Environmental Impact 20% Weight
Projects long-term public benefit, sustainability contribution, and commercialization potential.
